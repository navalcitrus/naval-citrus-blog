Lights Out on Surfaces

published 2026-10-14

  • #Topology
  • #Puzzles
  • #Algebra

In Lights Out the objective is to find a series of button presses which turn out all the lights on the board. The original formulation had lights in two states On or Off on a 5x5 grid where each button press caused neighboring lights to alternate state.

We can imagine a generalized version of this puzzle where lights can have many states, the board can have multiple dimensions and topologies, and button presses can have varied effects Below you can interact with many instances of the puzzle generalizations and mix-and-match rules. If you get stuck, I’ve also included a generalized solver (see if you can figure out how it works).

The source can be found on my GitHub .
